03

Who it's for & the problemFeeds the pain section above the method.

Dream client
A woman who used to love riding and now freezes at the mounting block. Maybe she had a fall, a bolt, or a bad scare. Maybe the nerves crept in with age. She still loves horses. She just can't get her body to trust the saddle again.
The problem in their words
"Everyone keeps telling me to just get back on. I try, and my body won't let me. I feel ridiculous being scared of something I used to do without thinking."
What they've tried
Pushing through it, groundwork drills, "confidence" clinics, calming supplements, or quietly stepping back from riding altogether. It manages the fear at best. It never clears it.

You're not broken, and it's not a nerve problem. The fear is a specific memory your brain linked to danger, and it's been honoring that link below your awareness ever since.
